Field Overview

What to expect at BAT Airsoft

Varied Terrain

From dense pine forest to barricaded urban killhouses, our maps shift every few rounds to keep scenarios fresh.

Beginner Friendly

New to the sport? We walk every beginner through safety rules, eye protection checks, and replica handling before game start.

Rental Packages

Don't own gear yet? Rent a reliable Specna Arms AEG, anti-fog face protection, and 1,000 BBs for game day.

Eye Protection Required Full seal ANSI Z87.1 eyewear must be worn at all times outside the safe zone.
Call Your Hits Airsoft relies on honor. When hit anywhere on your person or gear, call it loudly and raise a dead rag.
Mandatory Chrono Every replica is chrono checked with 0.20g BBs before entering the field. No exceptions.
