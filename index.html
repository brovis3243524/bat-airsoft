Strict Chrono Safety

Mandatory Joules check before entry. Equalized, safe, and honest gameplay guaranteed for all players.

EYE & FACE PROTECTION

ANSI Z87.1 rated eye protection required at all times outside safe zones. Full mesh or rigid lower face masks are mandatory for under-18s and strongly recommended for all players.

BANG RULE & ENGAGEMENT

Minimum Engagement Distance (MED): 0m for AEGs under 1.14J, 10m for DMRs (1.88J), and 20m for Sniper Rifles (2.32J). Call out 'BANG BANG' when within 2m behind unaware opponents.

HONOR & HIT CALLS

Airsoft is an honor sport. Direct hits to gear, weapons, or clothing count as a hit. Call 'HIT' loudly, raise your red dead-rag high, and proceed directly to respawn.
